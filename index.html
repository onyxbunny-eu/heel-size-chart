How heel height is measured

Measure vertically at the back of the shoe, from the floor to the point where the heel meets the shoe. Retailer methods can vary.

General comfort tips

Choose a secure fit with toe room, try new heels for short periods, and stop wearing a pair if it causes pain or numbness. General footwear information only, not medical advice.

Women's heel sizing FAQ

Are women's shoe size conversions exact?

No. Conversion charts are approximate because sizing and fit vary by brand, style and last. Check the maker's chart before buying.

How do I measure foot length for heels?

Stand with your weight balanced, mark the heel and longest toe on paper, measure the distance, and measure both feet. Use the larger measurement.

What should I do if I am between sizes?

Follow the brand's guidance and consider the toe shape, materials and fastening. A secure fit should not pinch or allow excessive slipping.

What counts as a high heel?

This guide groups heels over 7.5 cm through 10 cm, or roughly 3 through 4 inches, as high. Retailers may use different labels.

Does a platform change heel height?

The listed heel height is still measured at the back, but a front platform reduces the height difference between heel and forefoot.

Is this chart only for heels?

The women's size conversion can be used as a starting point for many shoe styles, but heel shape and fit can change how a size feels.
